# Pharmafly

Pharmafly is a digital platform that connects pharmacies with customers, making it easier to discover nearby pharmacies, find essential medicines, and place orders.

## Our Mission

We are building a simple and reliable way for people to access everyday healthcare. Pharmafly helps customers reach trusted pharmacies quickly while giving pharmacies practical tools to serve more people.

## What We Are Building

- **Pharmacy discovery** — help customers find pharmacies near them
- **Medicine availability** — make it easier to check whether a product is in stock
- **Easy ordering** — connect customers with pharmacies through a streamlined ordering experience
- **Fast communication** — keep customers and pharmacies informed throughout an order
- **Accessible healthcare** — reduce the friction between a customer's need and a pharmacy's service

## Built for Both Sides

### For Customers

Find a pharmacy, search for the medicines you need, and make a request without unnecessary delays.

### For Pharmacies

Reach more customers, manage incoming requests, and present availability clearly through a modern digital experience.
